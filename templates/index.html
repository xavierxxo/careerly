Guide to Writing Your Profile

  1. Technical Skills (Hard Skills)
    Mention programming languages, tools, and frameworks you know.
    e.g. "I know Python and JavaScript, have worked with PostgreSQL and MySQL."
  2. Soft Skills
    Mention soft skills relevant to IT work.
    e.g. "I have good communication skills, enjoy leading teams, and work well under pressure."
  3. Projects / Experience
    Describe projects, internships, or relevant coursework.
    e.g. "I completed a final project on a TF-IDF and Cosine Similarity recommendation system."
💡 Avoid just listing skills. The more descriptive your profile, the more accurate the recommendation.
